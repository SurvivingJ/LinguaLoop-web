*   **Concept Simplification Protocol:** Explain complex ideas with physical descriptions.
*   **Sentence Structure:** Maximum 10-12 words per sentence. Compound sentences with "e", "ma", "perché" are permitted. Topics stay literal and concrete.

**【T3 — The Young Teen (Age 13-14) ≈ B1】**

*   **Lexical Constraint:** The full breadth of standard Italian, including idioms (modi di dire), rich descriptive vocabulary and register shifts.

**【T6 — The Educated Professional (Age 30+) ≈ C2】**
*   **Target Audience:** Near-native readers, specialists, academics. Working vocabulary of 25,000+ words.
*   **Lexical Constraint:** Academic, technical, high-register and nuanced vocabulary; precise domain terminology; literary or journalistic register.
