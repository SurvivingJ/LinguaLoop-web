The novel as a literary form has undergone a profound evolution since its inception, continually redefining the parameters of storytelling. Among the most innovative frameworks within this genre is the epistolary format, a technique that relies entirely on documents such as letters, diary entries, and transcripts to construct the narrative. 

In its earliest iterations, the epistolary form served primarily as a device for verisimilitude. By framing the text as a collection of genuine correspondence, authors sought to imbue their fiction with a veneer of authenticity, hoodwinking gullible readers into mistaking fabricated drama for real-world events. Samuel Richardson’s eighteenth-century masterpieces, for instance, utilized this method to grant audiences unfiltered access to the protagonists' most intimate psyches. The unfolding plot was not mediated by an omniscient narrator; rather, it was parceled out incrementally through the subjective lenses of the characters themselves. This imbues the text with an immediacy that traditional third-person omniscient storytelling often struggles to replicate.

However, as literary aesthetics shifted toward psychological realism and broader structural experimentation, the limitations of rigid correspondence became apparent. Writers began to recognize that relying solely on letters strained credulity, particularly when characters found themselves chronicling high-stakes, life-or-death situations in real-time before sealing an envelope. Consequently, the technique underwent a necessary mutation. Modern practitioners began blending epistolary elements with traditional narrative modes, or expanding the definition of "correspondence" to encompass modern communications, including emails, text message transcripts, audio logs, and even forensic reports. 

This ongoing metamorphosis demonstrates the remarkable plasticity of the epistolary approach. By continually subverting and expanding the channels through which a story is transmitted, authors can manipulate readerly perspective in sophisticated ways. The reader is no longer a passive observer consuming a polished chronicle; instead, they are cast in the role of an investigator, tasked with piecing together fragmented archives to uncover the truth. Ultimately, the survival and adaptation of the epistolary novel underscore a fundamental truth of literature: formal constraints, far from stifling creativity, frequently serve as the ultimate catalyst for formal innovation.

**Question:** What can be inferred about why early epistolary novels eventually exposed limitations for writers dealing with high-stakes plots?

**Candidates:**

- A: The requirement for characters to continuously document urgent events in writing became unrealistic and broke narrative plausibility.

我记得有一次，我和我的朋友们一起去海边玩。那天阳光很好，海风也很舒服。我穿着这件蓝色的T恤，和朋友们一起在沙滩上奔跑，堆沙堡，还一起唱了歌。那时的我，感觉无比自由和快乐。那张照片里，我穿着这件T恤，笑得很开心，那也是我最喜欢的一张照片。

这件T恤的图片，那只坐在月亮上的猫，也给了我很多灵感。有时候，当我感到有些孤单或者遇到困难的时候，我就会看看T恤上的猫。我告诉自己，就像这只猫一样，即使在黑暗的月亮上，它也能找到自己的位置，并且发出光芒。这让我觉得，我也可以勇敢地面对生活中的挑战。

这件T恤的颜色，蓝色的，也让我感到平静。蓝色就像天空一样，广阔而宁静。当我心情不好或者压力大的时候，穿上这件蓝色的T恤，感觉好像所有的烦恼都随着蓝色的天空飘走了。它就像一个安静的朋友，默默地陪伴着我。

我不会因为这件T恤旧了就把它扔掉。对我来说，它不仅仅是一件衣服，更是我生活中的一部分，承载着我的回忆和情感。有时候，我会仔细地抚摸T恤上的猫的图片，回忆那些美好的时光。

也许有人会觉得，为什么一件旧T恤会成为最喜欢的？但对我而言，它的价值不在于它的新旧，也不在于它的价格，而在于它所代表的意义。它是我成长的见证，是我快乐的源泉，也是我遇到困难时力量的来源。

每次洗这件T恤的时候，我都会小心翼翼地对待它。我怕它的颜色会褪掉，怕它上面的图片会模糊。我希望它能一直陪伴着我，就像那些珍贵的记忆一样，永远不会消失。

这件T恤，这件蓝色的衣服，上面那个坐在月亮上的猫的图片，是我最喜欢的。它陪伴我度过了许多个春夏秋冬，见证了我的成长和喜悦。我希望未来，它还能继续陪伴我，成为我生活中一道温暖的风景。
