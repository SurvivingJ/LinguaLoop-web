Brand identity · round one

Ten marks for LinguaDojo

Brief from ADR-004 and your answers: rigour over comfort, no mascots or game-app gloss, abstract marks only (no ensō or hanko), built from an LD monogram, a correction mark or a loop. Studies 01–03 use your three palettes; 04–10 take pairings from nature.

01
LinguaDojo

Single Stroke

LD monogramDelft & DuneFraunces

One unbroken line draws the L's stem and the D's bowl. The D's top-left corner is left open so the L's stem stands clear. Cobalt on bone recalls Delft ink on unglazed clay, and the sand rule underneath is the mat the work happens on.

  • Cobalt#2346A0
  • Bone#EFE9DC
  • Dune sand#D2B98A

LinguaDojo

The Circled Word

Correction markPersimmonGloock

A line of text with one word circled in red by a teacher who was not satisfied with it. The ring overshoots its own start, the way a real pen does. It is the most direct reading of 'honest feedback' in the set.

  • Vermillion#DD3F26
  • Rice paper#F6EFE1
  • Lamp black#1D1A17

LinguaDojo

Inward Spiral

Loop / LD monogramMoss GardenYoung Serif

A D drawn twice, the second pass inside the first, so the line winds toward a point at the centre. It nods to LinguaLoop: each loop of practice is tighter than the last. Square corners keep it disciplined rather than playful.

  • Emerald#0D6A4A
  • Cream#F2EDDF
  • Deep moss#12291F

Breakout Tick

Correction markKingfisherSchibsted Grotesk

A tick that starts inside the D and breaks out through its edge: the moment you get something right that was beyond you. The kingfisher's blue back and rust breast give a pair of colours that is bright but not neon.

  • Kingfisher#0B6A86
  • Breast rust#E0782F
  • Riverbank#EDF0EA

Overprint

LD monogramLichen on SlateFamiljen Grotesk

A solid L and a solid D printed over each other like two passes of ink; where they overlap a third colour appears. It suggests two languages, or what you know plus what you're learning, producing something neither has alone. Heavy and geometric, so it holds up as a 16 px favicon.

  • Slate#2D3A41
  • Lichen#B8C46C
  • Overlap olive#6B7646
  • Field stone#E6E4DC

LinguaDojo

Insertion Caret

Correction markOxblood & OatInstrument Serif

The proofreader's caret (‸) means 'something belongs here'. It sits under a gap in a line of words, with the missing word placed above it. The vocabulary is an editor's, not a game's, which suits translators and test-takers.

  • Oxblood#6A1E2A
  • Oat#EBE0C9
  • Ripe wheat#C79A4B

LinguaDojo

Dial

LoopBasalt & OchreBricolage Grotesque

Three open rings with their gaps turned like a combination dial, and an ochre core. It echoes Vocab Dojo's rings and the idea of unlocking each one in turn. It keeps the current display face, so it is the least disruptive change to the live app.

  • Basalt#24221F
  • Ochre#C98A2A
  • Volcanic ash#E7E2D8

Linked Pair

LoopHeather & PeatNewsreader

Two loops woven through each other: your language and the one you're learning, held together by translation. It ties directly to dual translation and to practice as a loop. Moorland purple and peat-brown are an unusual, quiet pairing.

  • Peat#3A2D28
  • Heather#7F6AA2
  • Chalk#F1EDE6
09
LinguaDojo

Negative Space

LD monogramFjord & GlacierSyne

A solid tile with a D cut out of it. The ice-coloured D is the figure, and what remains of the tile is an equal-weight L. It is built to be an app icon first. A poppy-yellow full stop at the end of the L, echoed in the wordmark's underline, is the one point of warmth.

  • Fjord#14324A
  • Glacier#D5E4E8
  • Arctic poppy#E6C24A

LinguaDojo

Loop to Tick

Loop + correctionTidepoolRed Hat Display

One line that loops once, like a handwritten l, then drops into a tick. Practice, then the correct answer, in a single gesture. It combines both concepts you picked, and the colours come from a rock pool: kelp-green and anemone coral.

  • Kelp#1C3B36
  • Anemone#E46E55
  • Shell#F4ECE3

Wordmarks are set in live web fonts, so they are typographic sketches. The chosen direction would be redrawn and outlined before production. Each study is also saved as standalone mark.svg, app-icon.svg and lockup.svg files under wiki/design/brand/logos/.
