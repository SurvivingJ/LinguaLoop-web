Brand identity · round two

Five names, ten marks

The names recommended from the Latin and non-Latin rounds. Each gets two marks, two colour schemes and a phone mockup of the redesigned app. The first mockup shows the Today screen in light mode; the second shows exercise feedback after a wrong answer in dark mode.
