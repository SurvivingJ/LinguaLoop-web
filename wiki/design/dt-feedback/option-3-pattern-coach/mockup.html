How this page decides what to show
  • The checker almost never flags a correct sentence, but in Chinese it misses roughly half of small slips. Counts here are a floor, not an exact score.
  • A pattern appears only after 3 flags on 2 or more days, and enters focus after 5 flags in at least 10 chances.
  • We count per chance: a sentence whose reference uses a measure word is one chance to get measure words right.
  • "Under control" needs you to pass spaced retries of your own sentences, and a run of clean chances in new passages.
  • If a flag is wrong, say so on the sentence. It leaves your patterns immediately.
