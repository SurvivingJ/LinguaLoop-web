How these numbers work

  • The range counts only the words the vocabulary check draws from. Words LinguaDojo has not yet put into the check are not counted.
  • It measures recognition: choosing the right meaning from four options. It does not show whether you can produce the word.
  • If you choose “I don't know”, we count the word as not known. Guesses are corrected for the 1-in-4 chance of being right. If some of the words you skipped were partly known, the true figure is higher.
  • “Rated known after a direct check” is the app's own rating of a word you have answered directly. It is a model rating, not a probability, and reading tests can change it.
  • Some practice is not logged word by word and is not counted here: prestudy warm-ups, flashcard ratings, dictation and translation practice.
